Transitu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Transitu ("the game") is made by DocBernie Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

1. No account, no sign-in

You do not need to create an account or sign in to play Transitu. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: which levels you have finished, your results, coins, and your settings (such as language, sound and graphics quality). This information stays on your phone and is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this saved progress is removed.

3. Anonymous usage count

When the game is opened, it sends a small anonymous record to our server so we can count how many devices play the game and which versions are in use. The record contains only:

  • a random install code that the game creates on your device (it is not linked to you, your Google account or any other identifier, and a new one is created if you reinstall the game);
  • the game version (build number) and platform (for example Android);
  • your device model (for example "SM-S916B"); and
  • whether the optional Level 5 download is on your device (yes or no).

The same request also checks whether a newer version of the game is available. We use this information only to understand how the game is used and to improve it. We do not use it to identify you, for advertising, or to build a profile. It is stored on the servers of our service provider, Supabase. As with any internet request, the servers involved handle your IP address in order to send the reply; we do not use it to identify you.

4. Crash and performance reports

The game does not include its own crash-reporting or advertising tools. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may provide us with anonymous crash and performance information through Google Play. We use it only to improve the stability of the game.

5. Downloads through Google Play

Some game content (such as additional levels) is delivered through Google Play. Google handles those downloads under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive any personal information from that process.

6. Advertising and purchases

The current version of Transitu does not show advertising and does not sell in-app purchases. If we add either in the future, we will update this policy and the information in the store listing before the change takes effect.

7. Children

Transitu is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your information. The only information we receive is the anonymous usage record described above, which is not shared with anyone other than the service provider that stores it for us.

9. Deleting your data

Because the usage record is anonymous, we cannot tell which records belong to you. If you would like us to look into removing a record, email us the install code or device model and the date and we will do our best to help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at Bernardsakran@gmail.com.
