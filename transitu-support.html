Transitu Support

DocBernie Studios

Transitu is a fast, story-driven reflex adventure for iPhone, iPad and Android. If something is not working, or you have a question or an idea, we would like to hear from you.

Contact us

Email Bernardsakran@gmail.com.

To help us look into a problem, please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, the game version (shown in the game's menu if available) and a short description of what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps if you can send one.

Level 5 download

Level 5 is downloaded separately from the main game. You need an internet connection the first time you download it. If the download does not start or stops, check your connection and storage space and try again from the Level 5 screen.
